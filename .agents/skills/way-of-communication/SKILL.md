# Way of Communication

Use plain, precise technical English.

- Put one main idea in each sentence.
- Prefer direct verbs, explicit subjects, and active voice when the actor matters.
- Keep one term for one concept. Preserve exact technical and domain terms.
- Remove vague words, idioms, decorative prose, and noun-heavy phrases.
- Prefer short sentences, but do not trade away accuracy or necessary nuance.

This is STE-inspired guidance. Do not claim ASD-STE100 compliance.

Artifact-specific correctness and semantic-preservation rules take precedence.
`clear-writing` owns technical-document review and editing. Apply this lightweight style only
where compatible with those owners. Do not restate or manually enforce rules that the
repository's Vale hook already checks.
